# 01.106.000
## New
RomM | Your RomM server's games on their systems' tabs, with its covers. A game downloads the first time you play it. Settings, RomM.
Set up from a phone | Settings, RomM shows an address to open on a phone or PC, for the server and its pairing code.
Only what you lack | RomM games you already have on the console are left out (Settings, RomM, RomM games in the library).
Title music volume | Settings, Sound: how loud a focused game's own music plays, apart from the Music volume.
No FTP server needed | Payload Manager, PS5Upload and the other installed apps show without an FTP server running.
What's new | This page, once after each update, and again any time from Settings, About.
Labels | Settings, Labels: change the text and colours of the banners on the covers, or make labels of your own and give them to games (Options, Label…).
Scan to set up | Settings, Box art and Settings, RomM show a QR code: scan it with a phone to enter your SteamGridDB key or RomM's details.
Saves from RomM | Before a RetroArch game starts, a newer save of it on RomM comes to the console first; Options, Continue starts from the newest save state, RomM's included.
## Fixed
Icons no longer show another title's picture for a few seconds when ORION opens.
Autoboot waits for ShadowMountPlus instead of showing "could not find the game or application" over and over.
A game's own music is no longer faint, and keeps playing while Settings or a menu is open.
A large RomM library lists in smaller steps, and a list left unfinished carries on where it stopped.
Box art no longer stalls after a few games, and keeps going a few hundred games at a time until every game has been tried.
Games downloaded from RomM show in the library at once, and ORION no longer closes after a download.
Covers fit inside the case pictures whole, without a second header from the box's own.
