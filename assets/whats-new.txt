# 01.107.000
## New
Network drives | Settings, Network drives: games on a Windows shared folder or a NAS (SMB) show on their systems' tabs and copy to the console the first time you play them.
Install PS5 games | From a network drive: a game's folder, a .ffpkg or .exfat image, or a ZIP, 7z or RAR archive (in parts too), unpacked as it comes off the network, to the drive you choose.
Background picture | Settings, Appearance: a picture of your own behind the library in place of the sky.
## Fixed
Games installed on extended storage are looked for as well as the console's own.
ORION's helper tries again when the ELF loader is not ready yet, and says so when installed games cannot be read.
