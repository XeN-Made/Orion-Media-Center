ORION Media Center · for PS5

Every game you own,
one library.

A launcher for a jailbroken PS5: your PS5 and PS4 games, homebrew, media apps and emulated games side by side, with box art, favourites, tabs of your own and a galaxy glass look. Play and install games from a PC or a NAS, open the console's drives from a PC, and change everything in Settings, all with the controller.

Features

One library for everything

ORION finds what is on the console by itself and starts each title the way it should be started, emulated games straight in the emulator that plays them.

Library

Games, homebrew and media

PS5 and PS4 games, homebrew and media apps, and every backup ShadowMountPlus mounts (exFAT images included): /data/homebrew, /mnt/ext0/homebrew and USB drives.

Emulation

Start a game from its cover

Emulated games open directly in the emulator that plays them, no menus in between. When two emulators can play a game, ORION asks once and remembers.

Box art

Covers as you scroll

Box art downloads as the covers scroll into view: libretro's sets, GameTDB for Nintendo and XboxUnity for Xbox 360, or what PS5SX2, Porpoise and PS5-ECAM already downloaded. Pick your own picture, or choose from SteamGridDB's covers with a free key of your own.

Tabs

Arranged your way

All, Games, Homebrew, Media, Favourites, one Emulators tab with a row per system (or a tab per system), and tabs of your own, inside each other too: a tab's sub-tabs sit in a row under it. Each keeps its own view, sort, theme and default label. Tabs and sub-tabs.

Network drives

Games on a PC or a NAS

A Windows shared folder or a NAS (SMB) joins the library: its games show on their systems' tabs and copy over the first time you play them. Add a drive from a phone with a QR code, or let ORION find the drives on your network. Network drives.

Installing

Games and packages from a drive

PS5 games from a network drive as a folder, an image, or a ZIP, 7z or RAR (in parts too), unpacked as they arrive. PS4 and PS5 packages go to the console's installer, one after another; Activity (L3) shows the queue. Installing games.

Your PC

The console's drives on a PC

Share the console's storage and USB drives: open \\<console> in File Explorer and copy games and files both ways. The console on a PC.

Organise

Several at once, your own order

Tick several titles and move them together, carry a title to the place you want it in a tab, label any title, see each title's size and the free space, and sort by size. Installed apps and ELF programs can be deleted from their menu.

RomM

Your RomM server

Your RomM server's games on their systems' tabs, downloaded when you first play them, with RetroArch saves kept in step. Set it up from a phone with a QR code.

Themes

A look of your own

31 built-in themes, a theme per tab, theme files you write in a few lines of text, and a picture of your own behind the library (moving, too) with snow, rain or fireflies over it. Settings shows every change live. The theming guide.

Console

Made for the controller

Every setting in one place (R3), a first-run setup, English, German, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Hungarian or Swedish, background music and each game's own music, autoboot after the jailbreak, ORION again after rest mode, a return to ORION when a game closes, and updates that install themselves.

Side by side

Every kind of title in one tab

PS5 games, homebrew, and DS and GBA games in Favourites, each started with Cross: the emulated ones straight in their emulator.

Play time for every game and when you last played it, Continue playing over the All tab, favourites, hidden titles, search, and removing a title from the home screen (or deleting it) without leaving ORION.

Three views

Look at it three ways

Each tab keeps its own view and its own order: by name, recently added, platform, tag (Games, Homebrew, Media, under separators), recently played, most played, largest or smallest first, or your own custom order.

Network drives

Your PC's games, and the console on your PC

Add a Windows shared folder or a NAS by name or address, from the console or from a phone with the QR code, or let ORION find the drives on your network. Its games join their systems' tabs, PS5 games and packages install straight from it, and Activity (L3) follows every install.

The other way round, Share this console's drives opens the PS5's storage and USB drives in File Explorer on your PC.

Themes

See every change as you make it

Pick one of 31 themes, tune the sky, the glass, the colours and the corners, or give each tab a theme of its own. While you are in Appearance, Settings steps aside and the library shows each change live.

Themes are plain text files you can write, share and drop into /data/orion/themes/.

Install

Get it on your console

You need a PS5 you own, jailbroken, with a homebrew loader for folder apps, ShadowMountPlus loaded after each jailbreak, an ELF loader on port 9021 and an FTP server on the console.

  1. Download the latest release ZIP and extract it. It holds one folder, PPSA99062.
  2. Upload that folder whole to /data/homebrew/ with a plain FTP client in binary mode, so you have /data/homebrew/PPSA99062/eboot.bin. Some upload tools leave out files they do not expect in an app; copy every file.
  3. Wait about ten seconds. ShadowMountPlus registers the title and ORION appears on the home screen. If it does not, run ShadowMountPlus again.
  4. Open ORION. A short setup walks you through the language, the look, your tabs, games and labels, your emulators, box art and autoboot. The guide explains everything after that.

Coming from PPSA99061? ORION's title ID is PPSA99062 since 01.103.000. ORION 01.102.000 installs it beside itself as a new tile with a small NEW tag; on an older one, upload the PPSA99062 folder as above. Open the new ORION (your settings, favourites, box art and music are kept in /data/orion), then delete the old one from it: Options on its tile, Delete.

Updates

It updates itself

Each time it opens, ORION asks GitHub for the latest release, downloads a newer one in the background, checks it against its published SHA256 sum and asks before installing. Your settings, favourites and box art stay. What changed in each version.

Autoboot

Opens after the jailbreak

Off until you want it: turn on Autoboot ORION after the jailbreak in Settings, add the bundled orion-autostart.elf last to your autoloader's list (for example Payload Manager's Autoload), and ORION opens once nothing else is in front.

Help

When something goes wrong

Every launch, update and scan is written to a log in /data/orion. The README lists them, with the fixes for the usual problems. Still stuck? Ask, or report a bug, on the ORION Discord.
