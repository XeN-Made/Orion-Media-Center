Controls

The buttons, in the library

The bar at the bottom of the screen always shows what the buttons do where you are.

SquareStars the title as a favourite, or takes the star away.
TriangleSearch: type part of a name or a title ID. Circle clears the search.

R3Settings. Circle closes it.

The library

What you see on the home screen

ORION lists what ShadowMountPlus can mount and what the console has installed, plus the games your emulators can play, network drives' games and RomM's.

Views

Grid, Cases or List

Grid shows covers many to a screen; Cases shows big game cases three at a time; List shows a row per title. Pick it with Up from the covers, for every tab or for one.

Order

Sort the way you like

Name A–Z or Z–A, recently added, platform, tag (Games, Homebrew, Media under separators), recently played, most played, largest or smallest first, or your own Custom order (Move within this tab).

At a glance

Under the covers

The focused title's ID, version, kind and size, and when you last played it and for how long. Top right: how much space the console's storage has free.

Continue

Continue playing

Over the All tab, a row of what you played last. Up from the covers reaches it. Turn it off in Settings, Library.

Favourites

Starred and hidden

Square stars a title; Favourites holds every starred one. Options, Hide this title moves one to the Hidden tab at the end, and Show this title brings it back.

Your own look

A picture behind it all

Settings, Appearance, Background picture: a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP of yours (one that moves, too) behind the library, with snow, rain or fireflies over it if you like.

Tabs and sub-tabs

Shelves of your own, inside each other

The tab bar holds All, Games, Homebrew, Media, Favourites, Emulators (a row per system under it, or a tab per system) and tabs you make. Settings, Tabs makes new ones, hides, renames and reorders them, and gives each its own theme, cover shape, sort, view and default label.

Sub-tabs: a tab can sit inside another. Its parent then has a row under the tab bar: All first (everything in the tab and its sub-tabs), then each sub-tab. L2 and R2 step through them.

  • New tab in Settings, Tabs: after the name, ORION asks where it goes: on the tab bar, or inside a tab.
  • A tab's page, New tab inside this one: straight into that tab.
  • A tab's page, Inside: move an existing tab in or out.

One level deep: a tab with sub-tabs goes inside no other, and All, Emulators and Hidden hold none. A sub-tab whose parent you hide goes back on the tab bar.

Several at once

Tick, move, arrange

Select several (Options on a title): Cross ticks or unticks the focused title; you can change tabs and keep ticking. Options opens Move N titles to: Games, Homebrew, Media, your tabs or a new tab. Circle stops without moving anything.

Move within this tab (Options on a title): the direction buttons carry it through the tab (up and down by a whole row in Grid). Cross keeps it there, Circle puts everything back. The tab's sort becomes Custom order and remembers yours.

Activity

What ORION is doing, from anywhere: L3

Installs (with speed and time left), the package queue, box art (the game it is on and the next ones), RomM downloads waiting, and what has finished. Square clears the finished ones. While something runs, a small card shows over Settings too.

Network drives

Games on a PC or a NAS

Settings, Network drives. A Windows shared folder or a NAS (SMB) becomes part of the library: its games show on their systems' tabs and copy to the console the first time you play them.

  • Add a network drive: its address (\\PC\share or \\192.168.1.20\share), a user and a password. PC\user signs in to that PC's own account; an empty user is a guest.
  • Add one from a phone or PC: scan the QR code and type them there.
  • Find drives on this network: the PCs and NAS drives sharing folders, then the folders they share.
  • Look for games again: games sit in folders named for their systems (gba, snes, psx…), as ES-DE keeps them.
  • A drive's page: Install from this drive… and Remove this drive (its games leave the library; ones already copied stay).

Windows PC not found, or not opening? Set its network to Private (Settings, Network & internet), turn on network discovery and File and printer sharing (Advanced sharing settings), and share the folder. A Microsoft account signs in with its email address and password, not the PIN.

Installing games

PS5 games straight from a drive

A drive's page, Install from this drive…, lists what it holds. Pick a game and the drive it goes to (the console's storage, extended storage or a USB drive; one too full is greyed out).

What it isWhat ORION does
A game's folder (sce_sys inside)Copied whole.
An image: .ffpkg, .ffpfs, .exfatCopied, for ShadowMountPlus to mount.
An archive: ZIP, 7z, RAR (RAR5 stored or compressed)Unpacked as it comes off the network, nothing in between. A game folder inside is found wherever it sits.
An archive in parts: .part1.rar, .7z.001Every part read in order.

Activity (L3) shows the speed and the time left. Stop installing (Settings, Network drives) stops and deletes what was copied. A game that is already there is refused, not overwritten.

Packages

PS4 and PS5 packages, through the console's installer

In Install from this drive…, .pkg games, add-ons and updates show what they are and whether they are installed. Pick one and it goes to the console's own installer straight from the drive; it finishes in the console's Downloads. Pick more while one is going and they queue; Install all not installed queues every one a folder has. Add-ons for a game the console does not have say so. Needs kstuff, as any fake package does.

The console on a PC

Open the PS5's drives from File Explorer

Settings, Network drives, Share this console's drives, with a user name and password of your choosing. On a PC on the same network, type \\<the console's address> in File Explorer's address bar (Settings shows it) and sign in: the console's storage and its USB drives show as network folders, to copy games and files both ways. It keeps going after ORION closes, and starts again with ORION.

Settings (R3)

Every setting, section by section

L1 and R1 step through the sections; Cross picks a row; left and right change a choice. Each row's own description also shows under it on the console.

When something goes wrong

The usual problems

"ORION cannot reach /data"

ShadowMountPlus mounts /data into ORION. Update ShadowMountPlus, keep its backport_fakelib on, and turn BackPork off.

Games and installs do not start

ORION starts games, packages and sharing through an ELF loader on port 9021. If none runs, ORION says so: run your jailbreak's loader again.

A Windows PC is not found

Set its network to Private, turn on network discovery and File and printer sharing, and share the folder. Adding it by address works even when the search cannot see it.

Still stuck

Settings, About, Share your logs, and attach the ZIP on the ORION Discord (#bug-reports) or in a GitHub issue.
